| `fascia_r17.gcode.3mf` | front panel R17.gcode.3mf | 4 |

These are operator-supplied machine exports, distinct from the editable four-plate project and canonical STL/STEP geometry. The exported containers do not retain the model meshes required for an independent toolpath-to-CAD comparison. Their filenames/plate metadata establish the supplied identification; actual print quality and relationship of all settings to the intended manufacture remain unverified.

Publication copies remove the account identifier, filament-price fields and one price-only G-code comment. Every other G-code byte is preserved; no executable command is changed. The embedded G-code MD5 is recalculated and verified. All unchanged archive members are byte-identical to the received originals. Original and publication SHA-256 hashes, changed-member lists and checksum results are recorded in `verification/slicer_exports.json`. Unmodified originals remain in the external local backup.

No file has been sent to a printer or executed by this publication. Inspect the chosen printer, nozzle, filament, settings and layers before use; complete the relevant fit trials and acceptance sheets. Stored duration, material and layer estimates are slicer output, not observed printing results.
