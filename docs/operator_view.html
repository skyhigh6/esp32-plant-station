Plant Uno — wiring and button controls

Three illuminated buttons, a 16×2 LCD and a relay controlled pump. The knob chooses a 1.0–4.0 second run; below 25% moisture the red lamp blinks, and at 25% or above the green lamp stays on.

LCD backlight timeout uploaded to COM8. The display remains on the Uno 5 V rail; D10 is unconnected. UI v6 initialised the LCD with status 0, then logged backlight OFF after 60 seconds and ON after the knob moved. A pump run restarts the timer when it ends. Earlier LCD faults mean screen reliability still needs checking. The browser demo does not connect to the Arduino.

3 · Buttons and their built-in LEDs

Green · WATER

D2 → red switch lead; black switch lead → GND.

D7 → verified current limit → green LED+ lead; yellow LED− lead → GND.

Action: one press starts the 1–4 second run selected by the knob. The LCD shows a countdown, then returns directly to the soil percentage when the run ends. Releasing WATER does not cancel a run.

Light: steady when soil is 25% or wetter, including during a run; or on during all-lamp test. It is off during a STOP lockout or sensor fault.

Yellow · NEXT SCREEN / LAMP TEST

D3 → red switch lead; black switch lead → GND.

D6 → verified current limit → green LED+ lead; yellow LED− lead → GND.

Action: each idle press cycles Status → Raw readings → Session totals. Holding it lights all three button lamps. Pressing it during a run cancels that run and returns to soil status after release.

Light: dose in progress, or all-lamp test.

Red · STOP / cancel

D4 → red switch lead; black switch lead → GND.

D5 → verified current limit → green LED+ lead; yellow LED− lead → GND.

Action: press to cancel a run; the LCD returns to soil status. An idle press shows a STOP message. Hold for two seconds to clear completed/cancelled session totals.

Light: slow blink below 25% moisture, fast double flash for a rail-low sensor, solid during STOP/lockout, or on during all-lamp test. STOP is a software control, not power isolation.

The rear lead colours are user-reported for these buttons. For each physical button, confirm red/black are the normally open switch pair and green/yellow are LED+/LED− before applying power. Switch and LED pairs stay separate. The LED rating and any internal resistor remain to be verified.

4 · Existing LCD and analogue inputs

A4 / SDA→LCD SDA
A5 / SCL→LCD SCL
5V / GND→LCD supply / return, by backpack label
D10→Unconnected; do not wire LCD VCC to a GPIO
A0 / A1→Soil output / dose-pot wiper

Soil reading is advisory. The pot maps its lowest reading to 1.0 seconds and highest to 4.0 seconds; duration is not a calibrated water volume. Backlight control uses the LCD's I²C backpack; VCC remains at 5 V. The 60-second timer resets on button use, significant knob movement, and when a pump run ends. UI v6 initialised at LCD status 0; previous boots had intermittent faults. Check LCD supply stability, ground, A4/A5 and relay/pump suppression if it fails again.

5 · Relay stage Ratings open

D9 drives the relay module's logic IN. Your reported trigger is HIGH = ON. The operator reports that the pump and relay work; input current, boot-state behaviour, contact and pump ratings have not been measured here.

Verified 5 V coil supply → relay module
Uno GND → module logic GND as specified
Separate pump supply + → fuse/disconnect → COM → NO → pump +
Pump − → pump supply −

Use a relay module with a coil driver, not a bare coil on D9. Verify input current, coil supply, contact and pump ratings, suppression, and OFF through reset and supply sequencing. Provide a measured default-OFF input bias and a physical pump disconnect.

6 · Check the revised controls

T01
Verify all four leads with power removed.

For each button, red/black must be open released and closed pressed. Confirm green is LED+ and yellow is LED− from part markings or a current-limited test. Establish the LED rating and current limit before connecting D5–D7.

Record: button part IDs, continuity, LED polarity/rating, resistor decision and photograph.
T02
Upload and boot readback — passed this boot.

COM8 upload passed after the operator reported pump power OFF. Serial showed PlantUno UI v6, relay=OFF at rest and LCD initialisation status 0. Prior boots had intermittent LCD faults; measure power and I²C wiring if one recurs.

Record: port, sketch hash, serial log, rail voltage and LCD status.
T03
Check the three LCD pages.

Press yellow once per page: Status shows moisture and the selected 1.0–4.0 second run; Raw shows soil and knob ADC values; Session shows completed/cancelled counts and the last completed run. Holding yellow lights all lamps.

Record: screen photographs at minimum and maximum knob settings.
T04
Check control logic.

One dose per WATER press. At the instant the timed run ends, the LCD should return from the countdown to the Status page with soil percentage, even if WATER is still held. Red or yellow cancels a run; all buttons must be released before another. Hold red for two seconds to clear session counters. Confirm the knob only changes the next run once started.

Record: screen video at pump stop, minimum and maximum timing, cancellation and reset.
T05
Relay check with pump supply OFF.

Identify the module and verify its 5 V supply, input current, HIGH = ON, LOW = OFF, and contact state during cold start, reset and either supply-start order. Record any transient contact closure.

Record: module identifier/datasheet, measured values, contact state and test setup.
T06
Supervised 1–4 second trial.

The operator reports the pump works at 3.5 V. Measure actual low and high run times and delivered volume with the installed tubing. The pump rating and running/startup/stall current remain unknown.

Record: pump identity, measured current/voltage, timing and delivered volume.
T07
Soil lamp threshold — serial classification seen.

Serial reported DRY at 0%, WET above 25%, then DRY again below 25%. The physical lamps were not observed. With a valid sensor, hold below 25% for at least 500 ms: red should blink once per second and green should be off. At 25% or above, green should stay on. Raw A0 of 20 or less should give a fast double red flash and soil_state=FAULT. Confirm STOP/lockout makes red solid and lamp test lights all three.

Record: soil raw and percentage, observed lamp pattern, boundary crossing and sensor position.
T08
LCD backlight timeout — serial commands passed.

At status 0, serial logged LCD backlight=OFF idle just after 60 seconds. A knob movement logged LCD backlight=ON activity. Confirm visually, then finish a dose and check that the full 60-second period restarts at pump stop. Do not move LCD VCC to D10.

Record: LCD photographs and elapsed times. Pump-end reset and physical light change remain open.
