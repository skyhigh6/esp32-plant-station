## Shared visual template

A4 portrait; 40 mm left binding/history zone, 16 mm right margin; Helvetica body 9.5 pt/13 pt; numbered source sections; grey table headers and rules; black text and narrow black history lines. Illustrations retain aspect ratio and original technical meaning. Keep diagrams large enough to read. Title, revision control and LOEP precede the source content. Source technical status and cautionary language are preserved. No invented approvals, certification or completed tests.

LOEP lists every page at D01, because every page is newly restyled; it also maps source sections to current pages. D01 does not mean technical content was introduced at the latest design revision. Technical lineage is represented separately by the bars. Original page references must be updated to section references or the new page mapping; this is editorial only.

## Cumulative bars

R1 would be baseline with no bars, but no R1 output is created without its source. Use only the verified matrix entries. Attach a change ID and revision label to the affected surviving paragraph, table row or figure detail; do not mark an entire table or mixed illustration for one changed feature. Split mixed prose if necessary without changing its meaning. Narrow parallel labelled bars may occur beside content with multiple applicable historical changes. Preserve a prior label only for the surviving change; remove labels for superseded values/instructions. Changed figures with inseparable unchanged content are unmarkable at detail level: record in history instead of blanket-barring the figure. Pure movement, re-pagination, heading renumbering and D01 styling have no technical bar.

Verified means verified documentary comparison, not physical acceptance. An evidence row may be a technical design change, a new technical instruction/test criterion, an editorial explanation, formatting, deletion/supersession or unknown lineage. New evidence/status text can be technically material, but must never turn a CAD check into a physical pass.
